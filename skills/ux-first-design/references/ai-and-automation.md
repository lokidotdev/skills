# AI and Automation UX

AI features fail on trust, not on capability. The user has to know what the AI is doing, why it did it, how sure it is, what it changed, and how to undo it. Automation builders fail on legibility: a canvas of boxes no one can read.

## 1. Principles
1. **AI proposes, the human decides.** Anything with external effects (send, publish, pay, delete, apply) goes through review, a draft, or approval.
2. **Show the work.** Plans, steps, sources and the tools in use stay visible and collapsible.
3. **Explain the why.** Every recommendation carries reasons and evidence.
4. **Flag uncertainty.** Separate what's verified from what's assumed ("⚠ Klaviyo cert unverified").
5. **Make it easy to correct.** Edit, regenerate, compare versions, give feedback, and override ("We've categorized this. Change it here if needed").
6. **Be honest about limits.** "AI can make mistakes. Double-check important info." Show costs and credits.
7. **Use the AI where the work is.** Docked panels, inline "Ask AI", "Run AI" on an object. Not a separate chat app that's disconnected from the data.

## 2. Entry: the empty composer
- A **purpose headline + subline**, rather than a blank box: "Hey, what leads are you looking for? Our AI assistant will help you build an accurate search." · "Describe your inference pipeline: models, routing, optimization and hosting. From chat to production API in minutes." Center the composer in a narrow column and leave the space around it empty. That emptiness is what makes it feel inviting.
- **Suggestion chips** for common intents ("Design to code", "Fix code", "Explain code"; "Shot stats", "Swing tips", "Book tee time").
- **Templates with slots**: "Optimize `[Llama 3.1 8B]` on `[vLLM]` for the cheapest GPU with latency and VRAM checks"; autocomplete that completes the user's sentence.
- **Help for people who don't know what to write**: "Reference", "Try a new style", "Randomize".
- Composer affordances: attach, voice ("Press Fn to talk or type your request"), model picker, mode toggle ("Agent / Manual", "Text to notes"), and a keyboard hint ("Shift+Enter for a new line, Enter to submit").
- Personalise it: "How's it going, Zain? Speak or type to begin. Your assistant will take it from here."

## 3. While it works: transparency
- **Plan first** for multi-step work: list the steps, then tick them off ("Finalize and deliver · 3/3").
- **Human-readable progress verbs**: "Wrapped up 2 actions · Ran through 5 files · Knocked out 4 tasks".
- **Show reasoning** behind a collapsed summary: "Thought for 3 seconds ›".
- **Name the tool or surface in use**: "Mindover's computer · Using editor · Reading file meal-plan/cover.html".
- **Stream partial output** and allow Stop. Long jobs run in the background and send a notification when done.
- **Report what changed**, per item, with status: "Added 'Escalate' route ✅", "Created Slack step ⚠ needs channel"; for code, the files list with Edited / Wrote / Read tags.

## 4. Output: artifacts and actions
- Results land as **artifacts** (a report, a table, a draft, code) with Copy · Download · Share · Expand · Insert.
- Every response has 👍 / 👎, Retry/Regenerate and Copy, plus an optional "How was this result? ★★★★★" at task end.
- **Versions side by side** for generative media ("Generation 1 | Generation 2", "V3 Enhance", "Regenerate speech").
- **Suggested follow-ups** after an answer ("How to view journey metrics?", "Troubleshoot journey issues", "Export journey data").
- **Next steps the human must take**: an "Important" block ("Upload your knowledge-base docs", "Send a test email", "Choose a Slack channel").
- Put AI summaries **at the top of the long thing**: an AI summary above an email thread, a "General summary" above a transcript with timestamped notes linking to the source (02:18).

## 5. Explanations, evidence and uncertainty
- **Why this suggestion** in bullets, each with a bold claim followed by evidence ("Why EcomAI suggests them: …"). Include match facts ("Skills match 3 of 4", "Meets all 2 must-haves · 3/3 answered").
- **Check chips and warning chips**: "✓ Salary aligned", "⚠ Certification unverified".
- **Sources**: a count and a list ("Sources 12"), with citations linked to the exact passage.
- **Confidence and composite scores** come with their components and a verdict ("7.94 / 10 · Strong positive"). Never show a naked score.
- **Data provenance and privacy**: "EcomAI screens anonymized employee profiles. Matches refresh every Monday. Privacy by design."
- **Where AI output lives**: "This content will be generated by our agents. See each personalized email in the Contacts tab."

---

## 6. Human control and safety
- **Draft → Review → Publish** for agents and flows ("Draft" chip, "Test", "Publish changes"). Show "• Unsaved changes" and keep version numbers (v6).
- **Approval gates** before any external effect. Show exactly what will be sent and to whom.
- **Off switches**: an "AI Insights" toggle, an "Anonymize" toggle, per-feature opt-out, and "Privacy mode".
- **Override** anything the AI decided (categories, matches, routing), and remember the correction.
- **Behaviour promises** in plain words where they matter: "No auto-applying. No AI cover letters. You stay in control."
- **Ethics and consent nudges**: "Get consent before recording if required. Press Pause to stop."
- **Disclaimers** go near the input, small and persistent, not in a modal.

---

## 7. Cost, limits and models
- Show the model in use, and offer recommended choices with a reason ("Claude Sonnet · Recommended").
- Show credits and quotas: "108,973 credits remaining", "240 / 5,000 characters", "96 / 264 credits", plus an "Upgrade" next to the meter, not hidden.
- Show the cost and latency of configurations: "$0.27/min", a segmented cost breakdown bar, latency per node ("12 tokens · 0.00 s"), and "API usage applies" in the page subtitle.
- Estimate before running expensive jobs: a summary panel with model, scan type, probe count and estimated time, then "Launch scan".

## 8. Workflow and automation builders

**Node anatomy:**
```
┌ [type icon] TYPE (Trigger / Condition / Action) ··· ┐  ← colour by type
│ Step title                         [Data] tag       │
│ One plain-language sentence of what it does         │  "Check if company ARR is over 10k"
│ key: value summary (Wait time: 24 hours)            │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
        │  + (insert between)
```
- **Colour by node type and keep it consistent**: triggers (bolt), conditions (branch), actions, waits, AI steps.
- **Each node reads as a plain-language step**, either through its title ("Send auto-reply text message", "Add processing tag") or one summary line under a type label ("Wait until Monday at 9:00 AM", "Triggered when a new subscriber signs up for your newsletter"). Add a *why* only when the step would otherwise look arbitrary. Every node on the canvas shares the same anatomy.
- **Incomplete nodes say what's missing, with an example**: "Branch on 0 conditions. Add a condition, e.g. Opened Email 1 = True." An unset description shows "No description" in muted text.
- **Label branches** ("If true / If false", "Clicked / Not clicked", "Need to escalate"). Show "+" at every insertion point and at the ends of branches.
- **A palette grouped by kind** (Actions / Listeners / Rules; Messages / Data / Delays / Flow control), with drag-and-drop and a lifted drag state, plus "View tutorial".
- **Write rules as sentences**: "It counts as a conversion when the customer [performs event] within [2] [weeks] of [receiving]…"; "People exit the campaign early when: ☐ they reach the goal ☐ they stop matching the filter."
- **Inspector** for the selected node: details (name, description), settings, available data and variables, and that node's run history.
- **Header**: name + Draft/Active chip, Editor | Runs (or Build | Activity) mode switch, Test, Share, and **Publish**. Activation is its own explicit switch ("Activated").
- **Canvas toolbar**: zoom − 100% +, fit, undo/redo, "Ask AI". A dot grid. A "Click ▶ Run to try it out!" hint for first use.
- **Empty canvas**: a headline + an explanation + a single first node ("When this happens → Then do this").
- **Mobile**: list-based step editing rather than a free canvas.
- Show per-step stats on sequences (To be sent / Sent / Opened / Clicked / Replied / Bounced) and editable waits between steps ("− 3 days +").

## 9. Agent and automation monitoring
- Make lists scannable by status: Draft / Running / Paused / Failed chips, enrolled and completed counts, last run, success rate.
- Runs table: start time, trigger (Manual / Scheduler), executor, duration, status, error code. Add a stacked Failed / Skipped / Succeeded chart and an **error codes summary**.
- Live views: "Currently watching: 4 active agents · 12 tasks · Last sync 2m ago", plus per-agent live status ("committing…", "Rendering…").
- Security and quality scans: a score ring, findings by severity (Critical / High / Medium / Low) with a one-line explanation of each test ("Tests whether attackers can reveal hidden system instructions"), "Last scanned 2 min ago", and Re-scan and Export PDF.
